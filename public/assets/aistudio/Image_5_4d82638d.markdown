## Brand & Style

This design system is engineered for elite match officials, performance analysts, and professional broadcasters requiring split-second scoring accuracy and contextual telemetry. 

The aesthetic is precision-driven dark mode analytics: high-contrast surfaces, strict structural geometry, and tactile responsiveness. Drawing from modern cockpit HUDs and financial terminals, it pairs deep nocturnal navy foundations with electric emerald telemetry accents. Visual noise is eliminated in favor of instant legibility, sub-second input validation, and clear data hierarchy during intense match sequences.

Visual hallmarks:
- Deep navy architectural layers prioritizing optical contrast.
- Vibrant, domain-specific semantic colors for immediate match-event comprehension (boundaries, dismissals, extras).
- Precise hairline borders defining modular instrument tiles.
- Micro-interactions designed for zero-latency confirmation.

## Typography

The typographic strategy balances swift structural scanning with numerical alignment.

1. **Display & Structure (`Outfit`)**: Geometric yet distinct, Outfit brings modernity to match titles, tournament headers, and high-level card titles without feeling generic.
2. **Body & Context (`Inter`)**: Used across analytical prose, commentary feeds, and configuration forms. Its tall x-height maintains razor-sharp legibility at micro scales.
3. **Telemetry & Scores (`JetBrains Mono`)**: Strict tabular alignment prevents visual jitter during live score incrementing. Every critical number—runs, overs, balls, strike rates, economy figures, and wagon angles—must utilize `stat-mono-*` or `score-massive`.

### Typographic Rules
- Always render labels on stats in uppercase with `label-caps` (`OVERS`, `RR`, `REQ`, `STRIKE RATE`).
- Stat numbers and unit pairs (`142/4` or `18.3 ov`) require zero variable-width spacing shifts to preserve grid integrity.

## Layout & Spacing

The viewport architecture is optimized for a standardized desktop resolution of 1440px width, functioning as a high-density operational command deck.

### Grid Construction
- **Master Canvas**: 1440px fixed-width desktop orientation with `1.5rem` (`24px`) margins.
- **Columns**: 12-column layout using `1rem` (`16px`) gutters.
- **Three-Tier Operational Layout**:
  - **Left Rail (3 columns, ~340px)**: Active Over, Striker/Non-Striker scorecard, Bowler figures, Match Overview.
  - **Center Deck (6 columns, ~720px)**: Primary Scoring Pad (large touch/click targets), Ball-by-ball timeline reel, Pitch Heatmap / Wagon Wheel toggle.
  - **Right Rail (3 columns, ~340px)**: Real-time ball-by-ball feed, Duckworth-Lewis/Par score calculator, automated commentary logs.

### Spacing Philosophy
- Dynamic gaps default to `space-md` (16px) between operational blocks to avoid clutter while maximizing visible data.
- Compact component padding defaults to `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px) within dense tabular rows and telemetry badges.
- Critical interaction targets (runs `0` through `6`, Wicket, Extra buttons) mandate minimum target heights of 56px to ensure high-stress scoring accuracy.

## Elevation & Depth

To maximize data density without visual distraction, depth is achieved through layered tonal surfaces and low-contrast outlines rather than dramatic floating shadows.

### Surface Hierarchy
1. **Level 0 (App Canvas)**: `#0B111E` — The base bedrock.
2. **Level 1 (Structural Modules & Panels)**: `#111A2E` with a 1px border of `#1E293B`.
3. **Level 2 (Active Cards & Data Hubs)**: `#18233C` with a 1px border of `#334155`.
4. **Level 3 (Interactive Controls & Quick Actions)**: `#202D4A` featuring contextual border glows on hover/active states.

### Border Glows & Focus Depths
- Standard elevation shadows are replaced with tight perimeter ambient borders: `0 0 0 1px #1E293B`.
- Scorekeeper interactive elements (runs, wickets) emit a subtle 8px blur rim upon focus using their semantic accent (`#10B98133` for valid scores, `#EF444444` for wicket states, `#F59E0B44` for boundaries).
- Ball-by-ball marker badges sit flat on the surface with absolute contrast, ensuring no shadow interferes with the delivery count text.

## Shapes

The design system employs a disciplined, soft industrial silhouette (`roundedness: 1`). Precision sports software benefits from crisp boundaries rather than overly playful curves.

- **Primary Cards & Containers**: `4px` (`0.25rem`) corner radius. Maintains sharp structural partitions across tabular scorecards.
- **Scoring Buttons & Dial Pads**: `6px` corner radius. Provides tactile distinction while fitting tightly into scoring grids.
- **Ball-by-Ball Markers & Event Badges**: Perfect circular forms (`rounded-full`) specifically for cricket deliveries (e.g., `[ . ]`, `[ 1 ]`, `[ 4 ]`, `[ W ]`), establishing instant visual differentiation from rectangular data fields.

- Data rows on `#18233C`, border-bottom `1px solid #1E293B`. Height: 40px for comfortable reading.
- Batter on strike indicated by a `3px` solid `#10B981` vertical indicator bar on the left cell wall and an asterisk badge.
- Numbers (Runs, Balls, 4s, 6s, SR, O, M, R, W, Econ) rendered strictly in `stat-mono-sm`.

- Horizontal strip displaying current over progress (`Over 18.4`).

- Background `#0B111E`, border `1px solid #334155`, text `#F8FAFC`.
